# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Startup founders and technical decision-makers assessing Roman Travnikov's
AI automation, web architecture, or fractional CTO services. Readers also
visit the Insights articles and downloadable writing resources.

## Product Purpose

Explain the services, show evidence of the work, and help a visitor reach the
relevant service or contact page. Fix AI Writing gives readers an editing
reference they can download without registration.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, thoughtful. Use concrete examples and the author's actual
experience. The visual reference is the existing Ethereal Aura system in
docs/DESIGN_VISION.md, implemented through the tokens in src/global.css.

## Anti-references

Generic developer skill inventories, repeated boxed service cards, unsupported
performance claims, and the superseded terminal-style portfolio. Existing
brand decisions take precedence over a new visual theme.

## Design Principles

- Give each promotion a specific destination and a visible next action.
- Demonstrate a useful result before asking a visitor to download a resource.
- Keep long text readable over the animated background.
- Keep the existing service paths and content URLs intact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the existing keyboard navigation and skip-link patterns. Maintain readable
contrast, usable mobile layouts, and visible focus. Respect reduced motion.
See docs/DESIGN_VISION.md, src/global.css, and the existing layout components.

This context is derived from the repository's brand and acquisition documents
and the user's request to promote Fix AI Writing on the website.
